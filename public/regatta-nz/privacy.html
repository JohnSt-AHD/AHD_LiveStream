Privacy Policy

Regatta NZ · Altitude HD
Operated by ALTITUDEHD LIMITED
Last updated: 24 September 2026

This policy describes how the Regatta NZ mobile and web spectator app (“the App”) handles information. The App is provided by ALTITUDEHD LIMITED (trading as Altitude HD / AHD LiveStream).

What the App is

Regatta NZ shows regatta schedules, follow lists, and live course graphics for New Zealand rowing events. The Android app is a thin shell that loads remote content from https://ahd-livestream.vercel.app/regatta-nz/ (and related AHD LiveStream pages and APIs on the same host).

Accounts

The App does not require an account by default. You do not need to sign in, and we do not ask for a username, password, or email address to use the spectator features.

Information stored on your device

Preferences such as clubs or athletes you choose to follow, and related settings, may be stored locally on your device (for example via browser localStorage / WebView storage). This data stays on the device unless you clear app or site data.

Network and race data

The App needs a network connection to load content and refresh race information. Depending on the event setup, it may request:

  • Public or event race data from RowIT (or an AHD proxy of RowIT feeds)
  • Live course / computer-vision position data from AHD LiveStream APIs
  • Operator-configured settings (such as regatta code or livestream link) from AHD services

Standard technical logs (for example IP address, user agent, and request times) may be processed by our hosting provider (e.g. Vercel) when you load the App, as with most websites.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use the App to run advertising profiles or third-party ad networks.
  • We do not collect precise device location for the spectator App as a core feature.

Children

The App is aimed at spectators of rowing events. It is not directed at collecting personal information from children. Follow lists are stored on the device and are not used to create user accounts.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App or our practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact
