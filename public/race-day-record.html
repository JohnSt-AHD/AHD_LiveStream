Race-day record

Bookmark this page on the graphics PC (http://localhost:3000/race-day-record.html). Press Record at the start of the session. Each Draw (D) opens a race and stores the RowIT lane draw and competitors. Results (R) closes that race with official placings when the sheet is up. If you skip Results, the next Draw ends the previous race instead.
