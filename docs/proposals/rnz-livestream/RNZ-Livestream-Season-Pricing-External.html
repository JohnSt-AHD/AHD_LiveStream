Detailed technical specifications, host venue responsibilities, and payment schedule are set out in the Livestreaming Production and Service Agreement.

6. Notes

  • All fees are in New Zealand dollars and exclude GST.
  • Classic Season preserves last season’s event mix at a CPI-only uplift.
  • Maadi’s higher resource share is funded inside the package by a lower share for other events — Core and Classic totals are unchanged vs the standard CPI rebuild.
  • Packages above $200,000 receive a 50% discount on the amount above that threshold.
  • Additional or substitute events (including Oceania Coastal) are quoted separately if required.
