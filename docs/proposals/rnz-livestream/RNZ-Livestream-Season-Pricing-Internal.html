4. “Why is Core still ~$175k?” — cost pools

Fixed days did not shrink in the RNZ draft. NI Secondary goes 2 → 3 days, so Fixed is 14 → 15. Core-only is fewer events than last year’s paid six, not a lighter Fixed day load.

Pure day maths on Fixed alone after CPI is ~$157,719 (same days) or ~$168,984 (agreement). Core at $175k is the platform price with Maadi inside.
